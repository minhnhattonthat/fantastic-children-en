# Synopsis (for translators and reviewers; spoilers)

Source: the anime *Fantastic Children* (Nippon Animation, 2004; Takashi Nakamura) that the GBA game (Bandai, 2005)
retells, plus a read of the extracted script. The script decides wording; this file only says who is who.

## Setup
- For 500 years the white-haired **Children of Befort** (ベフォールの子供たち), led by **Soran**, with **Agi, Soreto,
  Hesma, Hasmodai, Tarlant, Mel**, have been seen across Europe, never ageing, reincarnating again and again,
  searching for a girl named **Tina**.
- **Tohma**, 11, lives on **Papin Island** with his Dad (who trains him in **Papin Kenpo**) and Mom. He finds the
  dreamlike picture-book visions of the prologue ("a mysterious dream landscape...").
- **Helga**, a silent orphan who draws a land with a crescent moon, and her gentle friend **Chitto** escape the
  **Chikao Children's Academy** (orphanage) to find the place in her drawings. Tohma helps them run from it.
- The game is a series of areas (islands, Chikao, Clermont, the GED facility, Gedling on Kuril Island, the spaceship
  Vega) with **Talk Battles** (Punch / Kick / Chop / Guard, Action Bar, Battle Items, Support Friends) and
  collectible **Record Shards** / **Memory Records** that unlock the past. Three endings by play time and collectibles.

## Reveals (do not give away early)
- Helga is the reincarnation of **Tina**, princess of the planet **Greecia** (daughter of King **Titas**). Tohma is
  the reincarnation of **Seth**, son of Titas's adviser **Goto**, betrothed to Tina; Tina loved **Soran**. Seth, in
  jealousy, killed Soran, then sent Tina's soul to Earth himself before dying.
- The Children of Befort are Greecian scientists whose souls followed Tina's to Earth to bring her home; their
  reincarnations made them the ageless "children".
- **Gherta** (Gherta Hawksbee) is Mel, who lost her memories. She leads the **GED** (ゲド機関), rebuilding the
  **Autozone**, which pulls souls back from the **Zone** (land of the dead) using **Orsel** (life force).
- **Dumas** manipulates the GED to put Tina's soul back into her old body. It fails.
- Ending: Helga chooses her life on Earth as Helga, with Tohma and Chitto; the Children's long journey ends.
