#### Important
- **s09E4BE** — 「本物をチットに見せたい」 means “I want to show Chitto the real thing”—a real snake, contrasted with its shed skin. “Show Chitto one!” instead suggests showing him a snakeskin and changes a wish into an instruction. Suggested EN: `SP "Shed Snakeskin" / Wish I could show Chitto / a real snake!`
- **s09E3E4** — 「かあちゃんの占いは当たるらしい」 means “Apparently, Mom's predictions come true.” “Mom gets it right?” turns this into a skeptical question and obscures what she predicts. Suggested EN: `SP "Fortune Kit" / Mom's fortunes come true, / they say.`
