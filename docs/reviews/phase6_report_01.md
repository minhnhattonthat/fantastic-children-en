#### Important

- **p0827F8** — 「きをつけないと あいつがいる・・・」 warns that they must be careful because he is there. “Careful, or he'll be there” incorrectly makes his presence a consequence of carelessness. Suggested EN: `We must be careful... / He's there...`

- **p08251E** — 「なかまをよぶと ショウヒされてしまう」 means summoning friends consumes Memory Records; it does not specify one per summon. “Uses one up” adds a fixed cost, despite the following explanation of individual Record costs. Suggested EN: `but summoning friends / consumes them...`

- **p081CB8** — 「宇宙船ヴェガ」 means “the spaceship Vega.” “Vega” drops the vessel designation contrary to the glossary; the destination-choice exception covers island names only. Suggested EN: `Spaceship Vega`

- **p08103A–p081056** — 「ハイッ！ お前のミライには / 大きな青い空が 見えるぞよ～！」 is Mom’s theatrical fortune-teller performance. Untranslated “Hai!” and an ordinary “I see” lose the mock-grand 「ぞよ～」 delivery. Suggested EN: `Aha! Your future...` / `A vast blue sky / reveals itself to me~!`

- **p081DA8** — 「はいっ！ 見えたッ！！」 means “Aha! I see it!!” in the same fortune-telling performance. “Mom: Hai!” leaves an ordinary Japanese interjection untranslated rather than conveying her showmanship. Suggested EN: `Mom: Aha! I see it!!`

- **p082BBA, p082BE0, p082C00** — 「いっこくも はやく デュマの・・・ / おそらくは ギリシアの宇宙船が / かくされた場所を つきとめなければ・・・！」 means they must urgently locate Dumas’s hiding place, likely where the Greecian spaceship is concealed. “Quickly, Dumas's... probably the Greecian spaceship...” leaves an unintelligible possessive fragment. Suggested EN across these entries: `We must find Dumas...` / `Find where the Greecian / spaceship may be hidden...` / `There's no time to lose!`
