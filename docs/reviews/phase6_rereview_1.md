#### Important

- **p082BBA / p082BE0 / p082C00** — 「デュマの・・・おそらくは ギリシアの宇宙船が かくされた場所」 means “where Dumas’s… probably Greecian spaceship is hidden.” The revision joins “find Dumas’s…” to “most likely where,” leaving the possessive dangling and misplacing the uncertainty. Suggested EN: `We must find where...` / `Dumas's ship, likely / a Greecian spaceship,` / `is hidden, as soon as / we can...!`

- **s09E4BE** — 「本物をチットに見せたい」 means “I want to show Chitto a real one”—a real snake, contrasted with its shed skin. “Chitto must see it” instead refers to the item and loses the joke. Suggested EN: `SP "Snakeskin" / Show Chitto a real snake!`

- **s09E3E4** — 「かあちゃんの占いは当たるらしい」 means “Apparently Mom’s fortunes come true.” “Said to come true” omits both Mom and her predictions, making the Fortune Kit itself sound like something that comes true. Suggested EN: `SP "Fortune Kit" / Mom's fortunes come true?`

- **p098510 / p098538 / p098562** — 「人生の中で めぐりあった 人たちと 心 かよわせ はぐくみ カタチつくるもの」 explains that a **soul** is nurtured and shaped through bonds with people encountered throughout life. The revision changes the subject to “hearts” and leaves “On the way to the soul one has now” conceptually muddled. Suggested EN across these rows: `The soul we have now` / `is nurtured and shaped / by bonds with people` / `we have met throughout / our lives.`
