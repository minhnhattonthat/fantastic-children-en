#### Important

- **p091376** — 「ケイジが もぐりこんだらしいぞ」 means “Apparently a detective sneaked in.” “A brat” mistranslates ケイジ and changes whom the guards are discussing. Suggested EN: `Looks like a detective / snuck in...`

- **p093A62–p093A8A** — 「いちばん 帰りたがって いたのに」「ここの いぞくに 引き取られる」 means that Hesma wanted most to return home, but his bereaved family here will receive his remains. “Taken in by family here” sounds like a living child's adoption and loses the tragic contrast. Suggested EN: `Hesma wanted to go home / most of all...` / `Yet his remains will go / to his family here...`

- **p092F78** — 「だから ソランと キミに 死んで つぐなう」 means “So I'll atone to you and Soran by dying.” “I'll die for Soran and you” omits atonement and can suggest a protective sacrifice. Suggested EN: `So I'll atone to you / and Soran with my death.`

- **p093768–p0937B4** — 「セス様 だったのね」「ずっと ティナ様の そばで まもっていたのね」 expresses Soreto's realization that Tohma was Seth and had been protecting Tina all along. “It was Seth-sama” obscures the direct recognition. Suggested EN: `Soreto: ...You were / Seth-sama all along...` / `...Always by / Tina-sama's side...` / `protecting her...`

- **p08FBB6, p08FE6A, p092360** — 「転生装置」 means “reincarnation device.” “Device” drops the identifying term despite the glossary's explicit no-abbreviation ruling. Suggested labels: `Reincarnation / device`; for p092360: `The reincarnation device / will send you to Hazama!`

- **p093AB6–p093AD2** — 「ヘルガと トーマの 前世の記憶は どうなるの？」 asks what will happen to Helga and Tohma's **past-life memories**. “Past memories... then?” loses both the distinction from ordinary memories and the clear question. Suggested EN: `Soreto: What will happen / to Helga and Tohma's` / `memories of past lives?`

- **p0908AC–p0908CE** — 「チカオ島で だっそうはんを おいかけていた 兵隊」 identifies soldiers who pursued escapees on Chikao Island. “These guys chased us” adds an unsupported identification of the escapees as the protagonists. Suggested EN: `Look! These are the / soldiers from Chikao Island!` / `They were chasing / escaped prisoners!`

- **p0913B6–p0913DC** — 「ほんきょくの 女ほうこくいん」 describes a female reporting officer from headquarters. “A reporter from the main station” suggests a journalist and broadcasting station. Suggested EN: `We caught a female / reporting officer from HQ` would abbreviate a term, so use `We caught a woman / reporting for headquarters` / `out front... You're with / her, aren't you?!`

- **p091DC2** — 「つまづいた チットに プラズマが のびる」 means plasma reaches toward Chitto **after he trips**. The omitted stumble explains why Wanda must make the fatal rescue. Suggested EN: `Chitto stumbled. / Plasma reached for him.`

- **p093886** — 「思いをこめて 見つめあった」 describes a gaze charged with feeling. “They gazed at each other” removes the emotional resolution after their ordeal. Suggested EN: `They gazed at each other, / their eyes full of feeling.`

#### Minor

- **p091F04–p091F2A** — 「しがみつき」「なき声で ワンダーを よび続けた」 means Chitto clings to Wanda and keeps calling his name through tears. “Clinging to Wanda, Chitto crying Wanda's name” lacks a finite verb. Suggested EN: `Clinging to Wanda, Chitto` / `kept sobbing his name.`

- **p091DEE** — 「さいごのカを ふりしぼり」 means “mustering his last strength.” “At his very last” is unnatural English. Suggested EN: `With his last strength, / Wanda...` followed by the existing continuation.

- **p093CC0–p093CDE** — 「私は ついに 見ることは ないだろうか」 wonders whether the speaker will ever see those shores. “Shall I never, never see them at last?” awkwardly combines “never” and “at last.” Suggested EN, following “your fair blue shores”: `shall I never` / `see them in my lifetime?`
