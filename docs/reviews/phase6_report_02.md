#### Important

- **p083990, p083A16, p084AF4, p084B9A, p084EBA, p084F60** — 「とくていの相手に」 means “against certain opponents.” “To 1 foe” / “to one foe” changes an opponent-specific effect into a single-target effect. Suggested EN: `Deals 5 damage to / certain foes.` / `20 damage to / certain foes.`

- **p083F1E, p083F5C, p0840CA, p0858B8, p085A7A** — 「だっそうはん」 identifies an escapee; it does not establish a criminal conviction. Repeatedly calling the elderly woman a “convict” adds an unsupported criminal history. Use `escapee` throughout. For p0858B8, also preserve 「おびえる」, “frightened”: `the escapee, an old woman / cowering in the bushes.`

- **p085594** — 「ヘルガはつくえにふせて」 means Helga was slumped over the desk. “Lay on a desk” puts her body on top of it. Suggested EN: `In the dark punishment / room, Helga slumped / over a desk,` followed by the existing `trembling...`.

- **p085A98** — 「ブソウそしきの あんやく」 means an armed organization’s covert activities. “An armed group at work” loses the secrecy and sinister implication introducing the conspiracy. Suggested EN: `and an armed group's / secret dealings...`

- **p0830C2, p083118, p083CBE, p083DCE, p084940, p084992** — 「宇宙船ヴェガ」 means “the spaceship Vega,” matching the glossary. `Vega` drops the designation; D8 permits dropping “Island” in destination choices but does not extend that exception to “spaceship.” Suggested EN: `Spaceship Vega`.

#### Minor

- **p082DBA–p082DE4** — 「いまを生きようとする ヘルガを思う キミのカが きっと ひつようになる」 says they will need Tohma’s strength and concern for Helga, who wants to live her present life. “Your strength, caring for Helga … will be needed” awkwardly makes the strength do the caring; “lives for today” also blurs the present-life distinction. Suggested EN across the two entries: `...Helga wants to live / her life here and now.` / `She'll surely need your / strength and care...`

- **p086BBA–p086BD2** — 「ヘルガの いしを 私たちは たしかめに来たの」 means they came to ascertain Helga’s wishes. “Helga’s wishes, we came to ask them” treats wishes as someone they can question. Suggested EN: `Girl: We came to learn` / ` what Helga wants...`
