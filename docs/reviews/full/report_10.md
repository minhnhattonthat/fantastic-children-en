#### Important
- p08B5A2 — 「ひとつ前の前世」 means the life immediately before Seraphine’s. “Another past life” loses that specific chronological relationship. Suggested EN across m615: `...This was dyed by / Christina, who lived / the life just before / Seraphine...`

- p08B9D2–p08BA6A — 「わからない なにかを／わからない だれかを まち続け」「とりつかれたように」 describes Seraphine waiting for something/someone she cannot identify and painting *as if* possessed. The English leaves “waiting” attached to “something unknown,” drops her inability to identify the person, and states possession literally. Suggested EN: `Like Helga, she waited / for something unknown / stirring in her memories... / for someone she could / not name... / As if possessed, / Seraphine kept painting / that landscape / again and again.`

- p08BB9C — 「ソウナンし かえらぬ人となった」 means he met disaster and died. “Was shipwrecked” introduces a wreck the Japanese does not specify; the following passage describes him diving after the handkerchief. Suggested EN: `Jim met disaster at sea / and lost his life.`

#### Minor
- p08C08E–p08C0B6 — 「光をさえぎるような どこか暗い場所を 探さなくては」 means they must search somewhere dark, sheltered from light. “A dark place, we must search” gives the speaker conspicuously unnatural word order. Suggested EN: `Yes... we must search / somewhere dark... / where the light / cannot reach...`
