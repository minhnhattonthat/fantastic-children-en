#### Important

- p095B92 — 「パパン島の さんばしに 小さな船が着いた」 means a small boat arrived at Papin Island’s pier. “A boat at Papin Island” removes the arrival that establishes the homecoming. Suggested EN: `A small boat docked / at Papin Island's pier.`

- p095EF2, p095FA4 — 「強く だきしめた／だきしめる」 means embracing tightly. Both translations omit the intensity of Seth’s embrace during the reveal. Suggested EN: `the youth held Helga tight.` / `There, holding Helga tight,`

- p0960DE — 「トーマに なりきれなかった」 means he could not fully become Tohma. “I couldn't be Tohma” loses the incomplete integration of Seth’s identity into his present life. Suggested EN: `...I couldn't fully / become Tohma...`

- p0961C2–p0961EA — 「すいへいせんを うめつくす『エンマ』の 巨大な やみのカタマリが せまっていた」 describes Enma’s immense mass of darkness filling the horizon and approaching. “over the horizon: ‘Enma,’ / a huge darkness, nearing” loses that scale and leaves the passage without a finite verb. Suggested EN: `"Enma" filled the horizon,` / `a vast mass of darkness / drawing ever closer.`

- p0967F2 — 「近すぎて・・・ 大きすぎて」 means Seth was too close, too large a presence in Tina’s life for her to recognize their feelings. “So close... so dear...” substitutes affection for that overwhelming presence and weakens the explanation. Suggested EN: `Too close... too great / a part of my life...`

- p096D80 — 「しんごうが とどかない」 means the signal is not reaching its destination. “No signal!!” instead suggests there is no incoming signal. Suggested EN: `Hasmodai: The signal / isn't getting through!!`

- p096F96 — 「ティナの時とおなじ」 means “the same as when it happened with Tina.” “That's like Tina's” turns a reference to the earlier incident into an unexplained possessive. Suggested EN: `Agi: Just like when it / happened with Tina...`

#### Minor

- p0958A0 — 「強く 生きて行かなくちゃ」 expresses resolve to keep living with strength. “Live strong” reads like a slogan rather than Helga’s quiet resolve. Suggested EN: `We have to stay strong...`

- p096BCA–p096BF0 — 「ふたりは・・・ トーマとヘルガは まだ戻らないのか」 asks whether the two still have not returned. “Are they... / Tohma, Helga: not back?!” is difficult to parse aloud. Suggested EN: `Agi: Tarlant! Those two...` / ` Are Tohma and Helga / still not back?!`

- p096E6A–p096E90 — 「トーマと ヘルガの シンパクが・・・ かんぜんに テイシした」 reports that both heartbeats have stopped. Singular “pulse... has” makes them sound as though they share one pulse. Suggested EN: `Tohma and Helga's hearts...` / `have stopped completely...`

### Assessment

Needs fixes — most dialogue preserves the characters well, but several omissions and awkward constructions weaken the homecoming, Seth’s reveal, and the final crisis.
