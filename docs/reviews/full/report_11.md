#### Important

- **p08DC78–p08DCA4** — 「ソランの手ににぎられたジュウを 自分のムネに あてた」 means Tina presses the gun **held by Soran** against her chest. “Tina took the gun” suggests she takes possession of it, changing the staging of her request that he kill her. Suggested EN: `Waking in bed, Tina / pressed the gun Soran / was holding` → `against her own chest.`

- **p08CE2A–p08CEAA** — 「その感情を引きがねに」「オエセルを りようした」「兵器としてキドウする」 describes emotions triggering a mechanism that uses Orsel to activate her as a weapon. “Tap the Zone, ... the ‘Orsel’ found there” muddles what powers the mechanism, while the trailing “a cruel mechanism” attaches awkwardly to the weapon itself. Suggested EN across these rows: `her feelings would / trigger a cruel mechanism / using "Orsel" found in / the Zone, world of death, / activating her as a mighty / weapon of destruction.`

- **p08D336** — 「人体兵器として きどうした」 means she **activated** as a human weapon. “Awoke” immediately after “lost consciousness” suggests she regained consciousness, obscuring the loss of agency. Suggested EN: `and was activated as / a mighty human weapon...!!`

- **p08DC2E** — 「オレが 人間として殺すか」 means “I kill her as a human being.” “As a man” introduces a masculine-duty reading into the deliberate contrast between scientists destroying an artificial body and a human killing another person. Suggested EN: ` ...or I kill her / as a human being...`

- **p08D888–p08D8B4** — 「わかった わかったよ」「もうなにも いわなくていい」 is repeated reassurance: “I understand, I understand; you don't need to say anything more.” The single “I know” followed by “Don't say any more” makes his comfort sound like an instruction to stop talking. Suggested EN: `Soran: I understand... / I understand, Tina...` → ` You needn't say / anything more...`

- **p08E2AC–p08E2CC** — 「遠く惑星ギリシアの 王女・・・ティナであったころの記憶」 identifies Tina as the princess whose memories Helga recovered. Ending the English with “a planet far away... Tina” leaves Tina grammatically detached and makes the reveal confusing. Suggested EN following “Helga regained”: `her memories as Tina...` → `princess of the distant / planet Greecia.`

#### Minor

- **p08CEC0–p08CEEA** — 「ギリシアの王であるワシの いかりとして」 means “as my wrath, the wrath of Greecia's king.” “As the King of Greecia, my wrath...” grammatically makes *my wrath* the king. Suggested EN: ` "I, the King of Greecia,` → ` shall rain that power / upon the land as my wrath...`

### Assessment
Needs fixes — several pivotal passages blur agency, the weapon's activation, and the emotional meaning of Tina and Soran's exchange.
