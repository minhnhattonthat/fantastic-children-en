#### Important

- **p092B58–p092B80** — 「すいへいせんを うめつくす『エンマ』の 巨大な やみのカタマリが せまっていた」 means Enma’s enormous mass of darkness filled the horizon and was approaching. “Over the horizon” loses its engulfing scale, and “a huge darkness, nearing” leaves the whole passage without a main verb. Suggested EN, coordinated with the preceding rows:
  - p092B12: `From that blue Orsel rift,`
  - p092B38: `a strange black thing...`
  - p092B58: `"Enma," a mass of darkness`
  - p092B80: `filling the horizon, / was drawing near.`

- **p093F30** — 「のばした左手の そでから アザが見えた」 means a mark became visible beneath the sleeve of the outstretched left arm. “A mark showed by a cuff” removes the specific location of the identifying mark immediately before Helga recognizes a past identity. Suggested EN: `A mark showed beneath / the outstretched / left arm's sleeve.`

- **p092F02** — 「おねがい セスッ！ トーマになって生きてッ！」 means “Please, Seth! Live as Tohma!” Dropping おねがい turns Helga’s desperate plea into another command, weakening this emotional turn. Suggested EN: `Please, Seth! / Live on as Tohma!`

- **p09398E** — 「こんどこそ 21回目の 人生を・・・」 stresses “This time, at last”—Agi’s determination to finally live the life they have been given. “This 21st life...” retains the count but drops that resolution at the conclusion of their repeated reincarnations. Suggested EN: `Agi: This time, at last... / In this 21st life...`

- **p0936D0** — 「まだ いしきは 戻ってないけど・・・」 means “She hasn't regained consciousness yet, but...” “She's still out” introduces slang into Soreto’s calm, adult reassurance, contrary to the established Befort voice. Suggested EN: `She hasn't woken yet, / but...`

- **p093CC0–p093CDE** — 「私は ついに 見ることは ないだろうか」 asks whether the speaker will never see their homeland’s shores. “Shall I never, / never see them at last?” introduces an unsupported repetition and awkwardly combines “never” with “at last,” obscuring the poem’s central question. Suggested EN:
  - p093CC0: ` shall I never`
  - p093CDE: ` see them?`
