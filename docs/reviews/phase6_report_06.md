#### Important

- **p0940B0** — 「白い じょうかとうが見える あの場所で」 means “at that place overlooking the white purification tower.” “Lighthouse” identifies the wrong structure; p085F5C explicitly describes its sea-purifying function. Suggested EN: `Where we could see / the white purification / tower...`

- **p09429E** — 「トーマの中で お前を みつめていた」 means Soran watched Tina **from inside Tohma**. “I watched you in Tohma” can put Tina inside Tohma instead. Suggested EN: ` From within Tohma, / I watched over you.`

- **p09490C–p094932** — 「やみの中から ふりかえると そこには トーマと ヘルガが いた」 means they looked back from the darkness and saw Tohma and Helga. “Turning in the dark, Tohma and Helga stood” incorrectly attaches the turning to Tohma and Helga. Suggested EN: `They looked back...` / `Tohma and Helga stood / before them.`

- **p094310** — 「それいじょうは なにも のぞむまいと」 expresses a resolution **not to wish for more**, rather than absence of desire. “And wanted nothing more” loses Soran’s self-restraint. Suggested EN: `I'd ask for nothing more.`

- **p094A28** — 「ともに 生きたい人たちを 強く 思うんだ」 means “think intensely of the people you want to live with.” “Think of them, hard!” drops the idea that Tohma immediately repeats in m1073. Suggested EN: `Think of those you want / to live your life with.`

- **p095272** — 「ここの いぞくに 引き取られる」 refers to being claimed by bereaved family here. “Taken in by family here” sounds like adoption and obscures Hesma’s death. Suggested EN: `His family here will / claim his remains...`

- **p0954A8–p0954C6** — 「私は ついに 見ることは ないだろうか」 asks whether the speaker will never see the homeland’s shores. “Shall I never, never see them at last?” combines incompatible expressions and disrupts the lament. Suggested EN: ` shall I never` / ` see them after all?`

- **p09559E** — 「宇宙船ヴェガ」 has the binding rendering “the spaceship Vega.” “The Vega” shortens the established term despite the no-abbreviations ruling. Suggested EN: `The spaceship Vega / began to move.`

- **p0957C4** — 「チット・・・ 私たちも 施設に戻りましょう」 is Helga’s gentle, inclusive suggestion to return together. “Chitto, back to the home” reads as a clipped order. Suggested EN: `Chitto... let's go back / to the home, too.`

- **p0967F2** — 「近すぎて・・・ 大きすぎて」 describes Seth’s overwhelming closeness and importance in Tina’s life. “Too close... too big...” sounds like physical size and damages the confession. Suggested EN: `You were so close... / such a part of my life...`

- **p09741E–p09748E** — 「せん年を生きた ヘビ」「うつくしい カゲロウ」「せんども よみがえらせようと」 describes a thousand-year-old serpent seeking to revive the beautiful mayfly a thousand times. The English attaches the age to the tears and drops the repeated resurrection and beauty. Suggested EN across these rows: ` The thousand-year-old / serpent sheds tears` / ` of love for the / beautiful mayfly,` / ` hoping to revive it` / ` a thousand times.`

- **p0974B0** — 「せんの いずみ」 means “a thousand springs.” “The springs” loses the poem’s linked repetition of a thousand years, revivals, and springs. Suggested EN: ` A thousand springs / gleam silver.'`

- **p0977DE–p097804** — 「クリスタルを あつめておけば いつか なかまになった時に たすけてくれるのかな」 wonders whether collected crystals will help **when the statue later becomes an ally**. “If I collect crystals, maybe it'll join and help” implies that collecting crystals recruits it. Suggested EN: `If I gather crystals now,` / `will it help me later, / once we're allies?`

- **p097A58** — 「この絵は あなたが 描いたんですね」 identifies Helga as the painting’s artist. “Yours, right?” asks about ownership, weakening the recognition that prompts her surprise. Suggested EN: `Young Man: You painted / this, didn't you?`

- **p097B30–p097B5A** — 「青年が…手にしようとした時 左手にある アザが 目に入った」 means Helga noticed his left-hand mark as he reached for the water. “As he took the water, a mark on his left hand” leaves the revelation without a main verb or observer. Suggested EN: `As he reached for water,` / `Helga noticed a mark / on his left hand.`

- **p098264** — 「ギリシアの さんげきは まだ続いている」 means Greecia’s **tragedy** continues. “Greecia’s grief goes on” changes continuing disastrous events into lingering sorrow. Suggested EN: `Greecia's tragedy / still continues.`

- **p098510–p098562** — 「人たちと 心 かよわせ はぐくみ カタチつくるもの」 says the soul is nurtured and shaped through heartfelt connections with people encountered in life. “Formed by the people met in life” omits that mutual connection, a central point of the closing narration. Suggested EN: ` Through the lives / that brought us here,` / ` the bonds we share / with those we meet` / `nurture and shape / the souls we have now.`
